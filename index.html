Two positioning options

Option A · Student-first

Accessible, practical and welcoming. Emphasizes finding a menu, café information and a comfortable atmosphere.

Provisional choice — best fit with our intended student audience, pending validation.

Option B · Premium specialty

Craft-focused storytelling, curated beverages and an elevated visual identity.

Tradeoff: could communicate quality but may distract from quick practical information.

Two persona hypotheses

These are imagined scenarios, not interviews or actual customer observations.

Between-classes student

Needs a quick menu view and clear practical information such as hours and whether study space is available.

Social café visitor

Wants to understand the atmosphere and whether the café suits a casual meetup with friends.
