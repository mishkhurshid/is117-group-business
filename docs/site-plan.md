# Tastee Cafe — proposed website (not a finished product)
## Pages
- Home: concept statement and navigation to menu
- Menu: illustrative drinks and pastries, with sample status clearly marked
- About: proposed café atmosphere and values, no fake origin story
- Visit / Contact: clearly marked placeholders until real hours, address and contact are known

## Initial design decisions
- Simple navigation, visible menu CTA and readable mobile typography
- Warm cream/espresso palette and original or licensed visuals
- Clear distinctions between conceptual mockup and actual business offerings
- No fabricated testimonials, prices, locations, inventory or verified study amenities

## Verification plan
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators
- Responsive checks at narrow and desktop widths
- Ask peer to find menu and locate caveats about sample offerings
- Record observed results with dates and links when testing occurs
