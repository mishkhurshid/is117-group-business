## Reviewer checklist
1. Open the original cited URL and confirm the specific finding.
2. Identify whether the finding is observation or assumption.
3. Follow the finding into a proposed design decision and planned check.
4. Comment on the issue with the result.
5. Author responds: implement, investigate, defer, or decline, with reason.
6. Link the changed documentation/code commit, check it, request reviewer retry.
7. Close only after the retry is recorded; leave unfinished issues open.
